## Frame-age / motion-compensation model (`--age`, `--odo`)
Points are seen `age` s ago and moved into NOW using the measured twist scaled by (1+odo), as the core does. When age > AGE_MAX the result is inop_age (STOP).
* `--age route --seeds 3` (measured route distribution, about 52 ms, clipped to 33..116 ms) gives the same pass/fail as ideal, with the same min clearances to within 0.1 cm.
* `--age 0.1`: same as ideal.
* `--age 0.1 --odo -0.3` (100 ms old points, twist under-read by 30%):
  * head-on 0.30 / 0.45 m/s rests at +2.8 / +2.3 cm (current) and +2.9 / +2.8 cm (prototype), i.e. 0.6 to 1.2 cm into the margin. No contact.
  * Everything else is unchanged.

## Current-core loophole found by the bench (code + synthetic + recorded)
`free_along` / `free_rotation` report the first margin hit at the FIRST sweep step (1 cm / 0.02 rad) even when the robot
is already inside the margin. When `need` is under one step (|v| < ~0.07 m/s, |w| < ~0.15 rad/s), `fr >= need` holds and the code
is **"ok", whatever the direction**, and the escape test is never reached.
* Wedge (leg 2 cm inside the footprint): v 0.06 forward, w 0.1 / 0.14 spin and (0.05, 0.3) all come out "ok" from the core and "collide" from the prototype.
* This is the wall_drift ratchet: 30 "ok" ticks plus 143 "escape" ticks creep from 3.5 to 0.2 cm.
